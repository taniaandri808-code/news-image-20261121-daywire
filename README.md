# The Day Wire — One World, One Day (21 November 2026)

What it is:
  • 35 dated news stories from Saturday 21 Nov 2026
  • KPI band at the top
  • Three illustrated lead stories
  • Numbered cards for quick reads
  • World map showing global coverage
  • ALSO-ON ticker of extra headlines
  • Sources footer identifying outlets

How to run:
  mkdir -p out
  MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py

Sources: BBC, Al Jazeera, The Guardian, NPR, DW, ABC Australia
